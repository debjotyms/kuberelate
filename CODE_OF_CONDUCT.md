# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

KubeRelate contributors, maintainers, and community members commit to making participation a
welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience regardless of age, body size, disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience level, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, caste, sexual identity and
orientation, or any other protected characteristic.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive.
- Give and accept technical feedback in good faith.
- Focus criticism on ideas and code, not people.
- Respect privacy and avoid sharing another person's private information.
- Acknowledge mistakes, correct them, and help others learn.
- Prefer clear, inclusive language and assume different levels of Kubernetes experience.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, intimidation, stalking, or discriminatory comments.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, personal attacks, sustained disruption, or deliberate humiliation.
- Publishing private information without explicit permission.
- Knowingly submitting credentials, private manifests, or other sensitive data.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in repository spaces and whenever someone publicly represents KubeRelate. The
maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when behavior violates this code. Enforcement decisions should protect affected
community members and remain proportionate to the behavior.

For a private conduct report, contact the repository owner through a private contact method listed
on the [@debjotyms GitHub profile](https://github.com/debjotyms). Do not include sensitive personal
information in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed with discretion, and retaliation against a
reporter is not acceptable.
